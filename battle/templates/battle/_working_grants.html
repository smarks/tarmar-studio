{% comment %} A Game Master's grant of a working to a figure (tarmar-studio #208). A canon's True Magic may give a working an effect that needs a grant: only a figure a Game Master has named the working for may craft it. Rendered from the battle's record (``Battle.workings_granted_json``), the one source of truth, so the reload a post causes is the whole update and nothing here appears or vanishes on a timer. Every viewer reads each grant with who granted it and when, as the battle log tells them (battle/_recorded_ruling.html, the row a lifted bound shares); only a Game Master running this fight (``runs_the_seats``) gets the forms, and the endpoint refuses anyone else. The grant form offers the figures who craft workings and the effects that need a grant, and takes any tier: a grant past the figure's tier cap is recorded, and its line says it waits on Channel being lifted. Included by the battle page and by the GM chat's Battle tab; each form names the page it came from (``next``). The battle payload carries this partial too (``rulings_html``), and battle.js puts it in place when ``data-record`` changes. The words are configuration (``battle.wording``, config/battle-rulings.toml). {% endcomment %} {% url 'battle:working-grants' battle.pk as working_grants_url %}
