# Your First Session

You have read a little, maybe rolled a character, and now there is a table
— real or virtual — waiting for you. This page walks through what a first
session of Tarmar actually looks like, and where in the world your story is
likely to begin.

## What a First Session Looks Like

Nobody starts by saving the world. A first session in Tarmar starts small
and close to the ground: someone owes someone money, a wagon hasn't come
back, a job is posted that nobody respectable wants. Your character — a few
years of working life behind them, more nerve than coin — takes the job,
pulls the thread, and finds out what it was attached to.

Expect three things from your first few hours of play:

- **A local problem** with one or two moving parts, small enough that
  first-year characters can out-think it, out-run it, or (carefully)
  out-fight it.
- **A choice with real stakes.** The interesting option and the safe option
  are rarely the same one. Take the interesting one; that's where the game
  lives.
- **Consequences that remember you.** What you did in your first session is
  who people think you are in your second. Reputations in Tarmar are built
  the slow way, starting now.

You do not need to know the rules to play. The GM handles the mechanics
until you want them; your job is to say what your character does and mean
it.

## Before You Sit Down

If you haven't yet, take the short path in
[Get Started](/tarmar/reference/get-started/): skim the
[Introduction](/tarmar/reference/introduction/), wander
[the world pages](/tarmar/reference/the-world-of-tarmar/), and
[create a character](/tarmar/characters/create/). Character creation gives
you someone with a home, a trade, and a few years of
[prior experience](/tarmar/reference/public-rules/characters/prior-experience/)
— which means you arrive at your first session already owing favors,
already known somewhere, already from somewhere. Use that. The best first
adventures grow out of who your character already is.

If your table has formed a [party](/tarmar/parties/), even better: the
threads below pull harder on a band than on a stranger alone.

## Where Journeys Begin

Most stories start on the twin isles of **Maja and Solberg** in the frozen
north — the settled kingdom and its wilder neighbor, described on
[The World of Tarmar](/tarmar/reference/the-world-of-tarmar/) page. Each
corner of the isles has its own kind of trouble waiting for someone new
enough to take it on:

- **Farport**, the kingdom's harbor town, where cargo moves day and night
  and the tallies don't always add up. Good first work for characters who
  can count, watch, and keep quiet.
- **Majahelm**, the walled seat of the crown, where the warhost is
  mustering and ambition costs the people under it. Work for characters
  drawn to soldiers, politics, or the space between.
- **Hurhelm**, horse country, where bloodlines matter and a broken promise
  between friends is a serious thing. Small matters that are not small to
  the family asking.
- **Seaside**, on Solberg's coast, where things wash ashore that match no
  manifest, and the town would rather handle them quietly. For characters
  who like questions with strange answers.
- **The roads and villages between**, where wells go bad, letters carry the
  wrong seal, and someone always needs a wall watched overnight. Trouble
  that finds you no matter where you're from.

Your GM will bring one of these threads to the table — or your character's
own past will supply one. Either way, you don't pick your first adventure
so much as answer it.

## For the GM

Bringing new players in? The tools on the GM side of the reference string
these threads together for you (GM access required):

- The [Hook Generator](/tarmar/gm/hooks/) rolls a ready-to-run hook matched
  to a party's experience, and shows its dice.
- [Hooks for New Characters](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/gm-tools/low-experience-hooks/)
  is the full set of first-adventure hooks, sized for level 1–2 characters,
  each anchored to a place above where one exists.
- The regional pages under
  [Maja & Solberg](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/the-world/maja-solberg/)
  carry the deeper lore behind each starting thread.
