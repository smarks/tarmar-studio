# Casting Spells

**Cost:** Spells cost mana equal to the spell's level.

**Roll:** 3d6 ≤ INT or WIS (depending on the spell)

Very low and very high rolls have special effects—some fortunate, some catastrophic. Your GM will tell you what happens.

**Some spells must hit:** Magic requiring hitting a target needs an additional DEX roll.

## Renewing Spells

Continuing spells require mana payment each turn during Phase 2 of combat. Unrenewed spells end immediately.
