# Characters

Characters in Tarmar are defined by their attributes, derived pools, background, and prior experience.

## Creating a Character

1. Determine your **Core Attributes** (STR, DEX, INT, WIS, CON, CHR)
2. Calculate your **Derived Pools** (Fatigue, Body)
3. Choose your **Background** (Provenance, Location, Wealth)
4. Optionally add **Prior Experience** to gain skills
