# Magic

## Mana Pool

Every character has a Mana Pool, built from the **Spell Points** earned through training in the Magic skill track. It starts at 0 and grows only with the Spell Points the Magic track grants (a roll of 8, 9 or 10 on its d20) — ordinary skill points do not convert into mana — and a Game Master may grant mana. There is no roll at character creation, and the total is **visible**: you always know your exact Pool.

Spells themselves are learned with skill points: see [Spell Mastery](spell-mastery). Level I in a spell lets you cast it, paying mana equal to the spell's level.

Mana is completely separate from other attributes. A clever weakling may have vast magical reserves; a brilliant scholar may have almost none.
