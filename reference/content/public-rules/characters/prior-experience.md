# Prior Experience

Characters start at 16 years of age and level 1. They can begin playing immediately or acquire additional skills through prior experience.

Prior experience is bought **a year at a time**. Play one year, or a run of
years in one go — a run simply repeats the year below and stops the moment a
survival roll fails.

For every year of prior experience:
- Survival roll required (death possible)
- Gain skill from track table (d20 roll)
- Gain XP based on track
- Earn money based on skill level
- Age (eventually suffering penalties)

## Experience Levels

Characters gain levels and skill points based on accumulated XP:

| Level | XP Required | Skill Points Every |
| ----- | ----------- | ------------------ |
| 1     | 0           | —                  |
| 2     | 2,000       | 1,000              |
| 3     | 4,000       | 1,334              |
| 4     | 8,000       | 2,000              |
| 5     | 16,000      | 3,200              |
| 6     | 32,000      | 5,334              |
| 7     | 64,000      | 9,143              |
| 8     | 128,000     | 16,000             |
| 9     | 256,000     | 28,445             |
| 10    | 512,000     | 51,200             |

Skill points are granted cumulatively as XP is earned. For example, at 3,000 XP you would have earned 2 skill points (at 1,000 and 2,000 XP).

Level 10 is the last row the table prints, but the grant does not stop there:
past 512,000 XP the level-10 rate keeps running, so a character earns one more
skill point every further 51,200 XP for as long as they keep earning.

See the subsections for details on Aging, Survivability, and Skill Tracks.
