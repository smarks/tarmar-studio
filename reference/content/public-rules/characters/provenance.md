# Provenance

Characters are grouped into social classes: **Nobility**, **Merchant**, **Commoner**

Roll percentile dice. This page used to let a player choose Merchant or
Commoner instead, and ask for the GM's sign-off on Nobility without a roll; the
ruling of 2026-09-28 overrides that choice: a player rolls provenance once and
cannot choose it, and only the GM sets it or rolls it again.
