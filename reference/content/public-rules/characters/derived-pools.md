# Derived Pools

## Fatigue Points

Fatigue tracks the depletion of physical or mental energy due to exertion, stress, or environmental factors.

- Fatigue = CON + WIS + INT + (DEX or STR, whichever is higher) + 2d6
- Normal hits reduce Fatigue only.
- At 0 Fatigue your character is unconscious. Below 0, survival saving throws begin — see [Injury Thresholds & Death](../injury-thresholds-death) for the exact thresholds (negative ½ to start, negative full to add a penalty).

## Body Points

Body points represent how many wounds a character can sustain before dying.

- Body = 2/3 x Fatigue (round up)
- Severe critical hits — a confirmed natural 20 (see [Attack Rolls](../combat/action-options/attack-rolls)) — reduce both Fatigue AND Body.
- At 0 Body your character is unconscious. Below 0, survival saving throws begin — see [Injury Thresholds & Death](../injury-thresholds-death) for the exact thresholds (negative ½ to start, negative full to add a penalty).

## Physical

Physical is a composite readout of bodily prowess, printed on the character sheet.

- Physical = ceil((Effective STR + Effective DEX + Effective CON) / 2)
- "Effective" means after aging penalties are applied — see [Aging](../prior-experience/aging). Effective DEX here does *not* include armor's DEX penalty (unlike the DEX used for aim/dodge).

## Mental

Mental is a composite readout of mental acuity, printed on the character sheet.

- Mental = ceil((Effective WIS + Effective INT + CHA) / 2)
- "Effective" means after aging penalties are applied — see [Aging](../prior-experience/aging). Charisma has no aging penalty, so its base value is used.
