# Turn Sequence

| Phase | Name                 | Order                      | Description                                                |
| ----- | -------------------- | -------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1     | **Initiative**       | 1d6 + adjDEX per combatant | Descending total; ties: higher adjDEX, then stable order   |
| 2     | **Renew Spells**     | DEX+INT+WIS (high→low)     | Pay mana to maintain continuing spells                     |
| 3     | **Initial Movement** | Initiative order           | Move or yield; movement stops upon engagement              |
| 4     | **Final Movement**   | Initiative order           | Those who yielded now move                                 |
| 5     | **Actions**          | adjDEX (high→low)          | Execute chosen action                                      |
| 6     | **Forced Retreat**   | —                          | Those who dealt physical hits (damage past the armour) and took none may push enemies back |

Each combatant rolls their own initiative: 1d6 plus their adjDEX modifier,
rerolled every turn. Phases 3 and 4 run in descending order of that total;
a tie goes to the higher adjDEX, and any tie still standing keeps a stable
order. This supersedes the older side-based roll in which the winner chose
to move first or second — that tactical choice survives as yielding: any
combatant may hold their Initial Movement and move in Final Movement
instead.
