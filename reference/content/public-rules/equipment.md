# Equipment

## Currency System

MU (Monetary Unit) is the standard measure of value. In most regions, 1 MU = 1 gold piece (gp).

- 1 MU (e.g. gp) = 10 Lesser MU (e.g. silver pieces (sp))
- 1 Lesser MU (e.g. sp) = 10 Minor MU (e.g. copper pieces (cp))

## Economic Context

- **Poverty line**: 10 gp/month (barely surviving)
- **Decent living**: 100 gp/month (comfortable middle class)
- **Daily survival**: ~0.3 gp/day (3 silver pieces)
- **Comfortable daily**: ~3 gp/day
