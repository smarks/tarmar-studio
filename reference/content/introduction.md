# Welcome to Tarmar

There is a world that is older than its own memory.

Its name is Tarmar, and it is a place of crowded harbors and quiet ruins, of crowded gods and quieter mysteries. Crops grow. Trade flows. Children are born, grow old, and die. For most of the people who live there, the world is exactly as large as the road between their village and the next market town — and that is enough.

But Tarmar is deep. It has layers most people never see, secrets most people never learn, and histories most people never suspect. This is not a flaw in the world. It is the whole point of it.

## A world that rewards curiosity

Tarmar is a fantasy world built for exploration and discovery. At its center stands the **Navel**, the greatest city ever built, and within it an ageless ruler who has shaped the fate of nations for longer than recorded history. Around that center the world turns — full of powers that do not explain themselves, mysteries no scholar can crack, and a magic that is real, observable, and never quite safe.

Who and what fills that world is yours to meet on the **[Explore the World](/tarmar/reference/the-world-of-tarmar/)** pages, or in the deeper **[The World of Tarmar](/tarmar/reference/the-world-of-tarmar/)**. This page is only the welcome.

## What it feels like to play

This is serious high fantasy — wonder and danger in equal measure. The system, evolved from *The Fantasy Trip*, stays out of the way until a decision matters, then tightens around the moments that count: tactical combat, meaningful choices, and characters who grow through play rather than by piling up numbers.

Tarmar is for players who want to *inhabit* a world, not just raid it. Who ask "why is this here?" before "what's the loot?" Who understand that the best stories come from choices with real stakes.

There are answers to the mysteries, if you look hard enough. Looking has costs, and the answers aren't always comfortable. But they are there, waiting, for those brave or foolish enough to look.
