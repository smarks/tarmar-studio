# Get Started

*Version 2026-09-04 23:07 EDT, merged to main in commit [bd9e50d3db5178942723366873f0c17f259fd4bc](https://github.com/smarks/tarmar-studio/commit/bd9e50d3db5178942723366873f0c17f259fd4bc) via [PR #284](https://github.com/smarks/tarmar-studio/pull/284).*

New to Tarmar? Here is the short path from "curious" to "playing." Take the steps in order, or jump to whatever pulls at you.

## 1. Read the Introduction

Start with the **[Introduction](/tarmar/reference/introduction/)** for a feel of the world: its tone, its mysteries, and what kind of stories it's built for. Five minutes here will tell you whether Tarmar is your kind of place. (It probably is.)

## 2. Explore the World

Wander the **[Explore the World](/tarmar/reference/the-world-of-tarmar/)** pages — the God King Emperor, the Dragons, the Wayfarers, the Pillars, and the rest. You don't need to memorize anything. You're just getting a sense of the place you're about to step into. For the deep, in-world version, read the **[The World of Tarmar](/tarmar/reference/the-world-of-tarmar/)**.

## 3. Create a Character

When you're ready to belong to the world rather than just read about it, **[create a character](/tarmar/characters/create/)**. The system (evolved from *The Fantasy Trip*) builds characters who grow through play, not by piling up numbers, so you don't need to optimize anything — just make someone you want to *be* for a while. You can manage all your characters from the **[Characters](/tarmar/characters/)** page.

## 4. Learn the Basics

You only need a little to start. Skim:

- **[Combat](/tarmar/reference/public-rules/combat/)** — turn sequence and your options on a turn.
- **[Magic](/tarmar/reference/public-rules/magic/)** — how casting works, and why reaching too far has a price.

Everything else you can learn at the table, in the moment it matters.

## 5. Play

Read [Your First Session](/tarmar/reference/first-session/) for what those first
hours at the table look like and where in the world your story starts. Then sit down with the **[Game Master](/tarmar/gm/)**, bring your character, and start asking the world questions. The best stories here come from choices with real stakes — so make the choice that's interesting, not just the one that's safe.
