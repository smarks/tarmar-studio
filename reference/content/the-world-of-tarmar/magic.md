# Magic

*Draft.*

Magic in Tarmar is real, powerful, and never quite safe. Reach for more than you can hold and the power reaches back — costing strength, senses, memories, or worse.

The wise treat it with respect, and try not to wonder too hard about what watches from the other side of the door.

*For the rules of casting, see [Magic](/tarmar/reference/public-rules/magic/) in the Reference. For the full account, read [The World of Tarmar](/tarmar/reference/the-world-of-tarmar/).*
