# The God King Emperor

*Draft.*

At the center of the world, in a tower on an island at the heart of the Navel, dwells the God King Emperor. No one agrees on what he is — a god, a deathless man, or something that was never human — but all agree he has ruled longer than recorded history, that his cryptic answers always prove true, and that he has never once lost a game played across centuries.

When he is kind, it is genuine. When he is silent, it is terrifying.
