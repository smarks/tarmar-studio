# The Pillars

*Draft.*

Scattered across the world stand ancient stone columns, carved with patterns no scholar has deciphered. With a [Wayfarer's](/tarmar/reference/the-world-of-tarmar/wayfarers/) help, a traveler can step into one and emerge a thousand miles away in an instant.

How they work, no one outside the Guild can say. Some Pillars are famous transit hubs; others wait hidden in forgotten places.
