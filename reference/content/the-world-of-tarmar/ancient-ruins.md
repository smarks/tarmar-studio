# Ancient Ruins

*Draft.*

In the deepest, least reachable places of the world stand ruins that belong to no one — made of materials no smith can name, built with techniques no mason can copy, marked with scripts no scholar can read.

No archive records who built them. The [dragons](/tarmar/reference/the-world-of-tarmar/dragons/), asked, only smile. They wait, silent and inexplicable, for someone to understand them.
