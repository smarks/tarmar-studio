# The Wayfarers

*Draft.*

The Wayfarers are a robed, secretive guild older than the kingdoms they operate within. They control passage through the [Pillars](/tarmar/reference/the-world-of-tarmar/pillars/) — and accept no money for it, only secrets, songs, memories, and whatever else happens to amuse them.

They know far more than they should, and explain none of it.

Most travelers' first brush with the Guild is nothing so grand as a
Pillar-crossing of their own — it's paying a toll on someone else's
behalf, in whatever strange coin a Wayfarer names that day.

*Read the full account in [The World of Tarmar](/tarmar/reference/the-world-of-tarmar/).*
