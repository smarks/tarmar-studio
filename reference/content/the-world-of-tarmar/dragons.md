# The Dragons

*Draft.*

The dragons are vast, ancient, and powerful — but what makes them truly terrible is that they *remember*: ages, cities, and wars that history itself has forgotten. They rarely speak, and when they do, they speak of things no one else can verify.

Ask them about the ruins that match no known civilization, and they only stare darkly at the questioner.
