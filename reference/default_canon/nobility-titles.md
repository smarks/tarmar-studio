# Nobility Titles

The ranks used in the kingdom of Eskarth, highest first. Most Game Masters need only a few of these; they are here so a lord's rank can be named consistently.

## The Crown

**King or Queen** — Rules the kingdom, commands its armies, makes its law and grants every other title.

**Prince or Princess** — A child of the monarch. The eldest is heir; the others hold estates or offices at the monarch's pleasure.

## The High Nobility

**Duke or Duchess** — Holds a great province and answers to the crown alone. There are few of them, and the crown watches each closely.

**Margrave** — Holds a march: a frontier province with the right to keep soldiers and the duty to hold the border. Ranks with a duke in law and below one at court.

**Count or Countess** — Holds a county, keeps its courts and collects its taxes for the crown.

## The Lesser Nobility

**Baron or Baroness** — Holds land directly from the crown or a great lord, in return for soldiers in war.

**Knight** — Granted the rank for service, usually with a small estate. The title is not inherited unless the grant says so.

## Offices That Are Not Titles

**Steward** — Runs a lord's household and estates. Often more powerful in practice than anyone the lord outranks.

**Reeve** — Keeps a town or harbour for its lord: collects dues, keeps the peace, hears small disputes.

**Warden** — Holds a bridge, ford, forest or fortress for its lord. Some wardenships are hereditary.
