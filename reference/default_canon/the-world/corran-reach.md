# The Corran Reach

The Corran is a short, wide river that runs west out of chalk hills, through a valley of farms and mills, and into the sea through a tidal estuary a mile across. The march that takes its name is the westernmost holding of the kingdom of Eskarth, governed for the crown by a margrave from his crag at [Brannock](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/the-world/corran-reach/brannock/). It is prosperous by frontier standards and poor by any other: salt and fish from the estuary, wool from the hills, flour from the valley mills, and the toll on every barge that uses the river.

## The Estuary

- **[Brinemouth](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/the-world/corran-reach/brinemouth/)** — the harbour town at the river mouth
- **[Lantern Rock](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/the-world/corran-reach/lantern-rock/)** — the light at the estuary's outer edge
- **[The Drowned Bell](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/the-world/corran-reach/the-drowned-bell/)** — a village the sea took, still visible at low tide
- **[Greyfen](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/the-world/corran-reach/greyfen/)** — the salt marsh south of the estuary

## The Valley

- **[Hollin Ford](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/the-world/corran-reach/hollin-ford/)** — the market town at the lowest crossing
- **[Brannock](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/the-world/corran-reach/brannock/)** — the margrave's seat
- **[Ostry Abbey](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/the-world/corran-reach/ostry-abbey/)** — a house of healers on a river island
- **[Wendle Mill](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/the-world/corran-reach/wendle-mill/)** — the guild mill village upriver

## The Hills

- **[The Tallow Hills](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/the-world/corran-reach/the-tallow-hills/)** — chalk downland, sheep and barrows
- **[Harrowdeep](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/the-world/corran-reach/harrowdeep/)** — a lead mine closed after a collapse
- **[Ashcombe](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/the-world/corran-reach/ashcombe/)** — the fort that holds the pass
