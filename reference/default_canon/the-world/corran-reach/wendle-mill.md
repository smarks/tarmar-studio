# Wendle Mill

A village of millers, carters and their families clustered round three great water-mills where the [Corran](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/the-world/corran-reach/) narrows below the hills. The mills belong to the Millers' Guild of Eskarth, which sets the price of grinding for the whole valley and is hated for it in proportion. The village itself is quiet, prosperous and close: outsiders are welcome to buy flour and not much else.

**Hooks:** Why has the guild's agent started measuring the weir? A small, ready-to-run start: [The Miller's Ring](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/gm-tools/low-experience-hooks/#the-millers-ring).
