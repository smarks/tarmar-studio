# Brinemouth

The harbour town at the mouth of the [Corran](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/the-world/corran-reach/), built on a spit of shingle between the river and the sea. Brinemouth lives on three trades: the salt pans along the southern shore, the herring boats, and the toll-house where every barge bound upriver pays the margrave's duty. Its harbour is closed each night by an iron chain winched across the entrance, a habit older than anyone's memory of why it began.

The town is run by a harbour reeve answerable to [Brannock](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/the-world/corran-reach/brannock/), and in practice by the salt-pan owners, who lend the reeve money. Factors from Port Ilvane keep a counting house on the quay and buy most of the salt before it is boiled.

**Hooks:** Who profits when the chain goes up late? What do the Ilvane factors want with the old warehouses they keep buying? A small, ready-to-run start: [The Night Chain](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/gm-tools/low-experience-hooks/#the-night-chain).
