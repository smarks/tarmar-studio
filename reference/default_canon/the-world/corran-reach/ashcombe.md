# Ashcombe

A stone fort and the straggling village around it at the head of the valley, where the road climbs out of the Reach and over the pass into the Uplands. Ashcombe exists to watch the pass and to collect the toll on Upland trade, and does both with a garrison of forty that ought to be sixty. Its captain is a capable soldier who has not been paid in two quarters and has started to say so in writing.

The border fair at Ashcombe each autumn brings clansmen down from the moors to trade ponies, amber and wool, and the garrison spends the week keeping the peace and the rest of the year talking about it.
