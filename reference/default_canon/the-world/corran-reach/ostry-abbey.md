# Ostry Abbey

A house of the healing order on a wooded island in mid-river, reached by ferry from the north bank. The brothers and sisters of Ostry keep an infirmary open to anyone who can reach it, a physic garden, and a library of herbals and older books that they do not lend. Their abbess is the best-educated person in the Reach and the only one the margrave consults without being asked.

The abbey is short of medicines every winter, and the order's rule forbids it to buy from anyone who will not give a receipt.
