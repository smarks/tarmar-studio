# Harrowdeep

A lead and silver mine in the eastern hills, worked for a century and closed twenty years ago after a collapse killed nineteen men. The headframe still stands over the main shaft; the spoil heaps around it grow a pale moss found nowhere else in the Reach. The mine's owners, a company in Hollin Ford, have tried twice to reopen it, and twice their surveyors have refused to go below the second level.

The miners who survived the collapse say they broke through into a hall that no miner cut, with a floor laid in squares and a door at the far end.

**Hooks:** What was behind the door? Who keeps relighting the lamp in the old pithead office?
