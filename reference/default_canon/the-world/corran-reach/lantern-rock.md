# Lantern Rock

A stack of black rock at the outer edge of the estuary, crowned by a tower whose fire guides ships past the sandbars. The tower is far older than the town; its lower courses are cut from a stone found nowhere in the Reach, fitted without mortar, and warm to the touch in winter. The keepers are a family who have held the post for five generations and are paid by [Brinemouth](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/the-world/corran-reach/brinemouth/) in coal, bread and silence.

The keepers say the fire was never lit by anyone before their great-great-grandfather. They do not say who kept the tower before then, or what the light was for.

**Hooks:** Why does the old stone stay warm? What does the keeping family know about the Lantern Kings that it will not tell the abbey?
