# The Tallow Hills

Rolling chalk downland north and east of the valley, open grass grazed short by sheep and crossed by drove roads older than the paved ones. Most ridges carry a barrow, a long grassed mound over a stone chamber. The shepherds leave a share of the first milk on the largest of them each spring and do not discuss it with priests.

The hill farms lie a morning's walk apart and earn little. Their people turn away strangers who come asking questions, and take in strangers caught by snow without asking any.
