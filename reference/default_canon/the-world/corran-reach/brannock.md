# Brannock, the Margrave's Crag

A castle on a limestone crag above the river, two hours upstream of [Hollin Ford](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/the-world/corran-reach/hollin-ford/). From its walls the margrave of the Reach can see the estuary to the west. The other end of his charge, the fort at [Ashcombe](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/the-world/corran-reach/ashcombe/), is two days' ride up the valley and known to him mostly by its captain's letters. The present margrave is old, careful and short of money; his heir is young, bold and shorter of it.

The castle holds the march's court, its treasury, its armoury, and a garrison that is smaller than the crown believes it is paying for.

**Hooks:** Where is the garrison money going? Who in the household is writing to the court in Eskarth, and what are they saying?
