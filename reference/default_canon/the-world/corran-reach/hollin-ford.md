# Hollin Ford

The market town at the lowest place the [Corran](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/the-world/corran-reach/) can be crossed on foot, where the coast road from [Brinemouth](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/the-world/corran-reach/brinemouth/) meets the old paved road east towards the capital. A stone bridge now stands beside the ford, built by the town and tolled by the ford-wardens, a hereditary office that sits in judgement on market disputes and keeps the stocks in the square well used.

Hollin Ford holds a market every seventh day and a wool fair at midsummer, when drovers from [the Tallow Hills](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/the-world/corran-reach/the-tallow-hills/) and traders from the Uplands fill every inn and most of the barns.

**Hooks:** The ford-wardens' office is up for inheritance, and two cousins want it. A small, ready-to-run start: [The Bridge Toll](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/gm-tools/low-experience-hooks/#the-bridge-toll).
