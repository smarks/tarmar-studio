# The Drowned Bell

A fishing village on the north shore of the estuary that the sea took in a single storm surge three lifetimes ago. At the lowest tides the stumps of its houses show above the mud and its church tower stands clear of the water to the belfry. The bell is still in it. Nobody has rung it since the flood, and fishermen from [Brinemouth](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/the-world/corran-reach/brinemouth/) will not set nets within sight of the tower.

The survivors' descendants live scattered through the valley and still call themselves by the village's name. Some hold that the storm was a judgement; others that the village had something the sea came for.

**Hooks:** What was in the church crypt when the water came in? Who is paying a Brinemouth boatman to row out at night? A small, ready-to-run start: [The Bell at Low Tide](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/gm-tools/low-experience-hooks/#the-bell-at-low-tide).
