# Greyfen

Salt marsh south of the estuary, miles of reed beds, mud flats and tidal channels that rearrange themselves with every spring tide. The eel-catchers who live on its few islands of firm ground know the channels by heart and share that knowledge with no one. The margrave's excise men consider Greyfen a smugglers' road; the eel-catchers consider the excise men weather.

Fog lies on the fen most mornings. Lights are sometimes seen moving in it where no channel runs.
