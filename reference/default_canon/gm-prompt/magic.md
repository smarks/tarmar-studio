## Magic System
Spells cost mana equal to spell level. Casting roll: 3d6 ≤ INT or WIS depending on the spell. Spells that must hit a target also need a DEX roll. Continuing spells are renewed with mana in Phase 2 each combat turn or end immediately.

### Schools of Magic
Common (0-70%): Elemental (water/fire/lightning/earth/wind), Passage, Protection, Mending. Less Common (71-100%): Weather, Counter, Arcane/True Magic, Control.
Spell lists by level — Elemental: 1 Missile, 2 Ball, 3 Bolt, 4 Shield, 5 Barrier, 6 Elemental. Passage: 1 Detect Magic, Light; 2 Knock, Hold, Blur; 3 Transparency, Detect Invisibility, Lock; 4 Breathing; 5 Flying; 6 Pass Wall; 7 Shape Change. Protection: 1 Counter 1, Shield, Detect Magic; 2 Counter 2, Shield Wall, Knowledge; 3 Counter 3, Minor Protection from Element; 4 Counter 4, Major Protection from Element; 5 Counter 5, Encase. Mending: 1 Heal, 2 Cure, 3 Web/Joining, Breaking, 4 Shaping. Weather: 1 Detect Weather; 2 Wind, Wind Counter; 3 Rain, Rain Counter; 4 Storm, Storm Counter. Control: 1 Persuade Minor, Calm, Enrage; 2 Minor Illusion, Fatigue; 3 Wound, Effect Mental State/Senses (Sleep, Confusion, Blind, Deafen, Laugh); 4 Persuade Major, Illusion; 5 Major Illusion, Area Effect, Effect Senses; 6 Force (Paralyze, Move, Breath, Run, Sleep).

### Spell Mastery
Level 1: can cast. Level 2: no hand gestures. Level 3: no verbal incantation; unlocks protection from the same spell (separate skill). Level 4: reduced mana cost; unlocks halt. Level 5: further reduced cost; unlocks reflect back onto the caster.
