## Magic System
Spells cost mana equal to spell level. Casting roll: 3d6 ≤ INT or WIS depending on the spell. Spells that must hit a target also need a DEX roll. Continuing spells are renewed with mana in Phase 2 each combat turn or end immediately.

### Special Casting Results
Very low and very high casting rolls have special effects, some fortunate and some catastrophic. Decide what happens; the public rules fix no table for it.

### Schools of Magic
Common (0-70%): Elemental (water/fire/lightning/earth/wind), Passage, Protection, Mending. Less Common (71-100%): Weather, Counter, Arcane/True Magic, Control.
Spell lists by level — Elemental: 1 Missile, 2 Ball, 3 Bolt, 4 Shield, 5 Barrier, 6 Elemental. Passage: 1 Detect Magic, Light; 2 Knock, Hold, Blur; 3 Transparency, Detect Invisibility, Lock; 4 Breathing; 5 Flying; 6 Pass Wall; 7 Shape Change. Protection: 1 Counter 1, Shield, Detect Magic; 2 Counter 2, Shield Wall, Knowledge; 3 Counter 3, Minor Protection from Element; 4 Counter 4, Major Protection from Element; 5 Counter 5, Encase. Mending: 1 Heal, 2 Cure, 3 Web/Joining, Breaking, 4 Shaping. Weather: 1 Detect Weather; 2 Wind, Wind Counter; 3 Rain, Rain Counter; 4 Storm, Storm Counter. Control: 1 Persuade Minor, Calm, Enrage; 2 Minor Illusion, Fatigue; 3 Wound, Effect Mental State/Senses (Sleep, Confusion, Blind, Deafen, Laugh); 4 Persuade Major, Illusion; 5 Major Illusion, Area Effect, Effect Senses; 6 Force (Paralyze, Move, Breath, Run, Sleep).

### Spell Mastery
Level 1: can cast. Level 2: no hand gestures. Level 3: no verbal incantation; unlocks protection from the same spell (separate skill). Level 4: reduced mana cost; unlocks halt. Level 5: further reduced cost; unlocks reflect back onto the caster.

### Push (Boosting)
Invest extra mana for greater effect. Pushing needs a Control Roll: 3d6 ≤ INT, with a penalty that grows with the mana invested. Fail badly enough, or roll an 18, and the spell slips the caster's grasp: Runaway.

### Channel
The more mana a caster channels into one casting, the harder it is to keep control; that is what the Control Roll measures. This canon sets no separate limit on Channel.

### Borrowing
A caster without enough mana may Borrow, drawing on reserves they may not have. Borrowing creates Debt, and Debt must be paid; the deeper the Debt, the more likely something goes wrong. Decide how it is paid.

### Runaway
The spell feeds on the caster: first mana, then, once the pool is empty and the caster has fallen unconscious, Body. When the Body fails, the caster dies. Anchor: the caster rolls 3d6 ≤ WIS each round to regain control. Another mage may try to help; if the intervention fails, both are caught in the Runaway. Survivors often bear marks: white streaks in the hair, strange scars, a haunted look.

### Counterspell
The defender commits mana and both make opposed INT rolls; the more mana committed, the better the defender's chance. Defender wins: spell countered. Caster wins: spell goes off, and only the caster can escalate on later turns; the defender gets one chance to stop each casting. A failed Control Roll during escalation can trigger Runaway.
