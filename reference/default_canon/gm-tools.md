# GM Tools

Everything for running sessions: the interactive tools built into the site, and random tables for people, jobs, ruins, towns, factions, trade and the first push into an adventure.

## Interactive tools

- **[Game Master chat](/tarmar/gm/)** — The AI game master: run scenes, roll dice and keep session notes.

- **[Battles](/tarmar/battles/)** — The battle simulator: hex arena, six-phase turns, every roll and decision shown.

- **[Bestiary](/tarmar/gm/bestiary/)** — Browse the creature catalogue by category, or generate stat variants.

- **[Encounter generator](/tarmar/gm/encounters/)** — Roll wilderness encounters by terrain, linked into the bestiary.

- **[Ruin generator](/tarmar/gm/ruins/)** — Generate a ruin's origin, occupants, rooms and treasure.

## Tables

- **[NPC Tables](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/gm-tools/npc-tables/)** — What a person wants, what stands in the way, and what they are hiding.

- **[Job Generator](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/gm-tools/job-generator/)** — Who is hiring, for what, and what goes wrong.

- **[Dungeons & Ruins](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/gm-tools/dungeons-ruins/)** — Ruin origins, occupants, room contents and treasure.

- **[Urban Intrigue](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/gm-tools/urban-intrigue/)** — Trouble in a town, and how the characters get drawn into it.

- **[Faction Generator](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/gm-tools/faction-generator/)** — Groups with goals, means and a weak point.

- **[Trade & Economics](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/gm-tools/trade-economics/)** — Goods, routes and what upsets a market.

- **[Hook Introduction](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/gm-tools/hook-introduction/)** — Ways to start an adventure: patrons, consequences, places, rumours and personal ties.

- **[Hooks for New Characters](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/gm-tools/low-experience-hooks/)** — Small, local adventures for first-year characters.
