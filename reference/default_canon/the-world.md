# The World

*This is the default canon: a small, generic world that ships with the app so the Game Master and the reference pages have somewhere to stand when no campaign setting is installed. Replace it with your own by pointing `TARMAR_CANON_DIR` at a directory laid out the same way: these pages, plus the `nav.json` that lists them for the reference sidebar and the `maps.json` that names the map images and the gazetteer behind them. Both files are required; without them the pages are not served and the Game Master's panel has no map. A canon also carries a `magic-rules.json`, the casting success tiers, Push, Channel and Borrowing numbers the battle code's magic-rules loader reads, a `character-rules.json`, the numbers character creation reads: the point buy, the dice, and the height, weight, provenance, location and wealth tables, and a `skill-rules.json`, the numbers skills and prior experience read: the tracks and their skill tables, the experience ladder, the skill costs, the survival roll, the aging table, the attribute modifiers, and the skill and attribute checks.*

The known world is the wreck of an older one. Long ago the Lantern Kings, a line of sorcerer-rulers whose names survive only on boundary stones, held every coast within a month's sail. They built roads that still carry traffic, raised barrows on every hill, and fell so completely that no one now agrees on how. What remains is a scatter of kingdoms, free towns and hill clans living among their ruins, each sure that the old power was greater than its own and each a little afraid of finding out how much greater.

## Regions

- **[The Corran Reach](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/the-world/corran-reach/)** — A frontier march on the western edge of the kingdom of Eskarth: one tidal river, the port at its mouth, a valley of mills and fords, and chalk hills full of old graves. The default starting region, mapped in detail.

## Beyond the Reach

These lands have no pages of their own. They are here so a Game Master has names to reach for when a story leaves the map.

- **Eskarth** — The kingdom the Reach belongs to. Its court sits three weeks' ride east, at a distance that suits both sides: the crown takes its taxes and its levies, and otherwise leaves the margrave to keep order as he can.
- **The Uplands** — Moor and pine beyond the pass at Ashcombe, home to hill clans who owe Eskarth nothing and say so. They trade wool, ponies and amber at the border fairs, and raid when the harvest fails.
- **Port Ilvane** — A merchant city across the western sea, governed by its shipping houses. Its factors buy the Reach's salt and wool and lend money to anyone in the Reach who will sign for it.
- **The Barrow Roads** — The Lantern Kings' paved roads, still the fastest way across country. Travellers use them by day and avoid camping on them by night, for reasons every carter can give and none can prove.

## Magic in the World

Magic is real, uncommon and distrusted. Hedge-wizards and abbey herbalists are tolerated; a caster who can bend fire or weather is watched. Most people have seen a spell once or twice and would rather not see a third. The scholars of the abbeys hold that the Lantern Kings drew more power than any living mage dares, and that the ruins are what drawing it cost.
