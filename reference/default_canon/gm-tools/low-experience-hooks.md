# Hooks for New Characters

Ready-to-use hooks sized for characters with only a **few years of [prior
experience](/tarmar/reference/public-rules/characters/prior-experience/)**:
level 1 or 2, a handful of skill points and less coin than confidence. Where
[Scenario Seeds](../../scenario-seeds) assumes a party that can carry a plot
with several sides, these assume a party that has just left home: local
stakes, one or two moving parts, and an opponent a first-year character can
out-think, out-run or, with care, out-fight. See [Hook
Introduction](../hook-introduction) for the general patron, consequence and
environment framework these draw on.

Each is anchored to a place in [the Corran
Reach](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/the-world/corran-reach/), and that place's
page points back here.

Prefer not to pick by hand? The [Hook Generator](/tarmar/gm/hooks/) rolls
one of these for you, matched to a party's experience, and shows the die
alongside the result.

## Using These

Each hook names the level band it is sized for. **Levels: 1** hooks ask for
nothing but nerve and a free afternoon; **Levels: 2** hooks assume a party
that has already survived something. Run any of them as written, or move
them to a place of your own. None needs a particular kind of character to
solve: a fighter, a new mage and a sixteen-year-old with one term of
labouring behind them all have a way in.

## Jobs & Debts

### The Night Chain

**Region:** [Brinemouth](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/the-world/corran-reach/brinemouth/)

**Levels:** 1–2

Brinemouth closes its harbour every night with an iron chain, and the man who
works the winch has broken his arm. The harbour reeve will pay a few coins a
night for someone to stand the watch and raise the chain at dusk. On the
second night a boat with no lantern comes up to the chain and someone aboard
offers a purse to have it lowered for a quarter of an hour. Whether the
characters take the money, refuse it or report it, somebody in town expected
the old keeper to be on duty, and wants to know who replaced him.

### The Miller's Ring

**Region:** [Wendle Mill](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/the-world/corran-reach/wendle-mill/)

**Levels:** 1

The miller's younger son lost his father's seal-ring at dice to a drover,
who has since gone up the valley with his herd. The father uses the ring to
seal the guild's grinding tallies on market day, four days from now. The son
will pay everything he has to get it back quietly. The drover is willing to
sell it, for more than the son has, and the drover is not the only one who
has noticed what a guild seal could be used for.

### The Bridge Toll

**Region:** [Hollin Ford](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/the-world/corran-reach/hollin-ford/)

**Levels:** 1–2

A cousin of one of the characters is in the stocks in Hollin Ford square
for three years of unpaid bridge toll, and will stay there until the debt is
paid or worked off. The ford-wardens will accept labour in place of coin: a
few days escorting their toll-takers on the east road, where the collections
have lately been coming in short. The shortfall is not the cousin's doing,
and the warden who is skimming it would prefer the characters not notice.

### Garrison Pay

**Region:** [Ashcombe](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/the-world/corran-reach/ashcombe/)

**Levels:** 2

The margrave's steward at [Brannock](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/the-world/corran-reach/brannock/) needs the quarter's pay
carried to the fort at Ashcombe, two days up the valley road, and does not
trust his own riders with it. The chest is lighter than it should be, and
the steward says nothing about why. The garrison captain will count it.
What the characters do when the count comes up short, with forty unpaid
soldiers watching, is the job.

## Local Trouble

### The Bell at Low Tide

**Region:** [The Drowned Bell](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/the-world/corran-reach/the-drowned-bell/)

**Levels:** 1–2

On a still night with no wind, the bell in the drowned village rang three
times. The Brinemouth boats have not put out since. The abbess at Ostry wants
someone to row out at the lowest tide of the month, climb the tower and look
at the bell. The tower is sound enough. The bell has been rung recently by
hand, the rope is new, and the crypt below the church is dry, which it has
no business being.

### The Count Comes Up Long

**Region:** [The Tallow Hills](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/the-world/corran-reach/the-tallow-hills/)

**Levels:** 1

A widow farming alone in the hills has eleven more sheep than she owned a
week ago, none of them earmarked. She wants to return them before anyone
calls her a thief, and needs help finding whose they are. The trail leads
to a farm whose owner insists he has lost nothing, and to a barrow on the
ridge above it with fresh turf on one side.

## Small Mysteries

### The Novice's Errand

**Region:** [Ostry Abbey](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/the-world/corran-reach/ostry-abbey/)

**Levels:** 1

The abbey's infirmarian sent a novice to gather the pale moss that grows on
the spoil heaps at [Harrowdeep](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/the-world/corran-reach/harrowdeep/), a day's walk into the
hills. That was five days ago. The infirmarian wants the novice found and
the moss brought back, in that order. The novice is alive, camped at the
pithead, and refuses to leave until someone else has heard what she heard
from the shaft.

### Eels and Excise

**Region:** [Greyfen](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/the-world/corran-reach/greyfen/)

**Levels:** 2

The margrave's excise officer is hiring guides and guards to find the
smugglers' landing in Greyfen. The eel-catchers who know the channels will
not help him, and will help the characters only if they can be persuaded
the smugglers will come to no harm. The smugglers are bringing in medicines
for Ostry Abbey that the abbey cannot buy openly, and the excise officer
knows it and is after something else entirely.
