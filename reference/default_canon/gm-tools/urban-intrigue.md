# Urban Intrigue

Towns are full of people who want things from each other. Roll for what is happening, then for how the characters walk into it.

| d8  | Situation                                          |
| --- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Two families are fighting over an inheritance      |
| 2   | A guild is squeezing out a newcomer                |
| 3   | An official is taking bribes, and someone can prove it |
| 4   | A preacher is drawing crowds the temple dislikes   |
| 5   | Goods are going missing from the warehouses        |
| 6   | A respected citizen has vanished                   |
| 7   | Someone is buying up property through agents       |
| 8   | A foreign visitor is not who they claim            |

| d6  | The characters are drawn in because...      |
| --- | ------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | They are hired by one side                  |
| 2   | They witness something they should not have |
| 3   | They are mistaken for someone else          |
| 4   | Someone they owe asks a favour              |
| 5   | They are blamed for it                      |
| 6   | Their lodgings are in the middle of it      |
