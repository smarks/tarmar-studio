# Trade & Economics

What moves along the roads and rivers, and what disturbs it.

## Goods

| d8  | Cargo                                  |
| --- | -------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Grain or flour                         |
| 2   | Salt or salted fish                    |
| 3   | Wool or cloth                          |
| 4   | Timber                                 |
| 5   | Metal: lead, iron, silver              |
| 6   | Wine or beer                           |
| 7   | Medicines and herbs                    |
| 8   | Luxuries: spices, dyes, glass          |

## Market Disturbances

| d6  | This season...                                  |
| --- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | A bad harvest doubles the price of bread        |
| 2   | A new toll on the river or road                 |
| 3   | A glut: one good is suddenly cheap everywhere   |
| 4   | A shipping house fails and its debts are called |
| 5   | War or raiding closes a route                   |
| 6   | A guild fixes its prices and dares anyone to undercut them |

## Ventures

Characters with money to spare can buy a share in a cargo, a mill or a ship. A share pays out at the end of a season if the venture prospers and is lost if it fails; the tables above say what can go wrong, and the Game Master decides what a share costs and what it returns.
