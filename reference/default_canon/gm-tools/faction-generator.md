# Faction Generator

A faction is any group that wants something and will act together to get it. Roll once on each table.

| d8  | Kind                                  |
| --- | ------------------------------------- |
| 1   | A noble house and its retainers       |
| 2   | A guild or trading company            |
| 3   | A religious order                     |
| 4   | A band of outlaws                     |
| 5   | A circle of mages or scholars         |
| 6   | A village or town council             |
| 7   | A secret society                      |
| 8   | A mercenary company                   |

| d6  | Goal                                       |
| --- | ------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | Wealth                                     |
| 2   | Control of a place, road or trade          |
| 3   | Revenge on another faction                 |
| 4   | Protection of its members                  |
| 5   | Knowledge or a relic                       |
| 6   | Respectability it does not yet have        |

| d6  | Weak point                                       |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | Its leader is ageing and the heirs disagree      |
| 2   | It owes money to the wrong people                |
| 3   | A member is informing on it                      |
| 4   | It depends on one supplier, patron or secret     |
| 5   | Its members are split over the goal              |
| 6   | It has broken the law and can be proven to have  |
