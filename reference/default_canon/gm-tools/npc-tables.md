# NPC Tables

Quick tables for a person the characters have just met. Roll once on each, then decide how the results fit together.

## Motivation

| d8  | Wants                       | Held back by                  |
| --- | --------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| 1   | Revenge for an old wrong    | An oath they cannot break     |
| 2   | Money, or the safety it buys | A debt already owed          |
| 3   | Standing among their peers  | A family that needs them      |
| 4   | To get out of where they are | Their own reputation         |
| 5   | To keep a secret            | Someone who already knows it  |
| 6   | To beat a rival             | Too few friends or resources  |
| 7   | To learn the truth of something | An injury or illness      |
| 8   | To be left alone            | A duty that will not let them |

## Manner

| d6  | Meets strangers with...                 |
| --- | --------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Open friendliness, until crossed        |
| 2   | Courtesy that gives nothing away        |
| 3   | Suspicion that has to be earned past    |
| 4   | Impatience; they are busy               |
| 5   | Flattery, because they want something   |
| 6   | Fear, of the characters or of someone else |

## Secret

| d6  | They are hiding...                          |
| --- | ------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Money they should not have                  |
| 2   | A past under another name                   |
| 3   | That they work for someone the characters know |
| 4   | A crime, theirs or a relative's             |
| 5   | A gift for magic they have never admitted   |
| 6   | Nothing; they are exactly what they seem    |
