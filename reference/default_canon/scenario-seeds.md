# Scenario Seeds

Adventure seeds for a party that can carry a plot with more than one side to it. Each names a place in [the Corran Reach](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/the-world/corran-reach/), a situation, and a table of things that may be true. Roll or choose before play; the players should not be able to guess which.

---

## Seed 1: The Margrave's Heir

**Region:** [Brannock](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/the-world/corran-reach/brannock/)

The margrave's heir has borrowed heavily from the Ilvane factors in [Brinemouth](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/the-world/corran-reach/brinemouth/), and the factors have begun asking for the debt to be paid in favours rather than coin. The heir hires the characters to find out what the factors actually want before he has to give it to them.

| d6  | What the factors want                                  |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | The toll on the river, farmed out to them for ten years |
| 2   | Mining rights at Harrowdeep                            |
| 3   | A marriage into the margrave's family                  |
| 4   | A document held in the abbey library                   |
| 5   | The Lantern Rock keepers removed from their post       |
| 6   | Nothing; someone in the household forged the letters   |

**Complications:** The old margrave does not know about the debt. The steward does, and has his own reasons for keeping quiet. The factors' agent lies about nothing except whom she answers to, and she has bought out three Brinemouth households this year without a single lawsuit.

## Seed 2: What the Mine Found

**Region:** [Harrowdeep](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/the-world/corran-reach/harrowdeep/)

The mining company in Hollin Ford has hired a new surveyor, a quiet foreigner who has gone below the second level and come back up. She wants guards for a longer descent and pays in advance.

| d6  | What lies beyond the door                         |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | A tomb of one of the Lantern Kings                |
| 2   | A library, dry and intact                         |
| 3   | A prison, still occupied                          |
| 4   | A workshop where something was being built        |
| 5   | A second door, and a third                        |
| 6   | Nothing at all, recently emptied                  |

**Complications:** The surveyor works for someone other than the company. The survivors of the collapse do not want the hall opened, and one of them is a ford-warden. The abbey has a book that describes the hall, and will not lend it.

## Seed 3: The Border Fair

**Region:** [Ashcombe](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/the-world/corran-reach/ashcombe/)

The autumn fair at Ashcombe goes ahead although the garrison is unpaid and short of men. A clan chief's son is killed in a brawl on the second night, and the clansmen want the killer handed over by dawn. The captain asks the characters to find who did it before he has to choose between the clan and his own soldiers.

| d6  | Who struck the blow                                        |
| --- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | A soldier of the garrison, drunk and frightened            |
| 2   | Another clansman, settling a quarrel from home             |
| 3   | A Hollin Ford horse-trader who was being cheated           |
| 4   | Someone paid to start a war on the border                  |
| 5   | The dead man's own brother                                 |
| 6   | No one; he fell, and the wound came after                  |

**Complications:** The clansmen will not wait past dawn. The garrison will not hand over one of its own without orders from Brannock. The true story suits neither side.
