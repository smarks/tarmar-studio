# Counterspell

A caster may try to counter an enemy's spell by committing mana in a contest of will. The defender commits mana and both make opposed Intelligence checks; the more mana committed, the better the defender's chance.

If the defender wins, the spell is countered. If the caster wins, the spell goes through, and the caster may try again next turn, escalating the duel. Only the caster may escalate; the defender gets one chance to stop each casting.

These duels can drain both sides quickly, and a failed Control Roll during escalation can trigger [Runaway](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/magic-mechanics/runaway/). See the public [Counterspell](/tarmar/reference/public-rules/magic/counterspell/) page.
