# Mana Pool

Every spell is paid for from the caster's Mana Pool. The pool is built from the Spell Points a character earns by training in the Magic skill track; ordinary skill points do not turn into mana. A new character's pool starts at 0, with no roll at character creation, and grows only as the Magic track grants Spell Points or when a Game Master grants mana.

The pool is visible: players always know exactly how much mana their characters have. Casting a spell costs mana equal to the spell's level. The public rules say nothing about spent mana coming back, and the app leaves a spent pool as it is until a Game Master sets it.

The public rules leave a casting roll's very high results to the Game Master. On the battle board this default canon settles them (the `casting_failure` block of its `magic-rules.json`): a natural **13** or more fails the spell whatever the caster's attribute. A failed casting keeps its mana, except on a **13** or a **14**, which spend it anyway; a **14** also starts a [Runaway](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/magic-mechanics/runaway/).

Mana has nothing to do with any attribute. A clever weakling may have a great deal and a brilliant scholar almost none. See the public [Magic](/tarmar/reference/public-rules/magic/) page.
