# Runaway

When magic slips from control, it feeds on the caster. The spell destabilises, and the caster's reserves drain away: first mana, then life. Each round the caster tries to regain control with an **Anchor Roll** (3d6 against Wisdom), and each round the task grows harder.

When the pool is empty the caster falls unconscious and the magic begins to consume the caster's Body. When the Body fails, the caster dies.

Another mage may try to intervene and help. If the intervention fails, both are caught in the spell.

Those who survive often carry marks: white streaks in the hair, strange scars, a haunted look. See the public [Runaway](/tarmar/reference/public-rules/magic/runaway/) page.
