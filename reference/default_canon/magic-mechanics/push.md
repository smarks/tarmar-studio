# Push

A caster may put more mana into a spell than its base cost, for a greater effect.

Pushing is dangerous. It needs a **Control Roll** (3d6 against Intelligence), and the penalty grows with the power the caster is trying to hold. Fail badly enough, or roll an 18, and the spell slips out of the caster's grasp entirely: that is [Runaway](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/magic-mechanics/runaway/).

See the public [Push (Boosting)](/tarmar/reference/public-rules/magic/push-boosting/) page.
