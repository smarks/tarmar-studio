# Borrowing

A caster without enough mana for a spell may try to Borrow: to draw on reserves they may not have. Borrowing creates **Debt**, and Debt must be paid. The deeper the Debt, the more likely something goes wrong.

The public rules say no more than that. On the battle board this default canon settles it (the `borrowing` block of its `magic-rules.json`): a caster short of mana spends all they have and borrows the rest, up to **4** owed in all. After the spell, they make a **Debt Roll**: 2d6 against Wisdom, less 2 for each point owed; a 12 always fails, whatever the margin, as a Backlash. A failed Debt Roll costs by how far it missed: by 1 or 2, a **Flicker**, costing nothing; by 3 to 5, a **Fray**, costing 2 Fatigue for each point owed; by 6 or more, a **Backlash**, costing 1 Body for each point owed. The points owed are all the caster owes at the roll, after any mana handed back. The spell has already resolved, and stands. Mana handed back to a caster pays the Debt before it refills the pool, and Debt not paid stays owed on the character's record after the fight.

See the Borrowing section of the public [Push (Boosting)](/tarmar/reference/public-rules/magic/push-boosting/) page.
