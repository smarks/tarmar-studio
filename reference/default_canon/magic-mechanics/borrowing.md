# Borrowing

A caster without enough mana for a spell may try to Borrow: to draw on reserves they may not have. Borrowing creates **Debt**, and Debt must be paid. The deeper the Debt, the more likely something goes wrong.

The public rules say no more than that. How Debt is paid and what goes wrong are the Game Master's to decide in this default canon.

See the Borrowing section of the public [Push (Boosting)](/tarmar/reference/public-rules/magic/push-boosting/) page.
