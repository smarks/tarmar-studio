# Magic Mechanics (GM Reference)

How the risks of casting play out at the table. Each page restates what the public [magic rules](/tarmar/reference/public-rules/magic/) tell players, and nothing more: this default canon adds no hidden numbers. Where the public rules are silent, the Game Master decides.

- **[Mana Pool](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/magic-mechanics/mana-pool/)** — the reserve every spell is paid from
- **[Push](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/magic-mechanics/push/)** — putting more mana into a spell than it costs
- **[Borrowing](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/magic-mechanics/borrowing/)** — casting on mana you do not have
- **[Channel](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/magic-mechanics/channel/)** — how much mana a caster draws at once
- **[Runaway](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/magic-mechanics/runaway/)** — when a spell slips out of control
- **[Counterspell](/tarmar/reference/dm-rules/magic-mechanics/counterspell/)** — one caster stopping another
